Questions

Can I go back to my old Phone app?

Yes, any time: Android Settings › Apps › Default apps › Phone app, and pick the one you had. Dial Deck then stops reading your call history and works as a widget only.

Why does it ask for these permissions?

As your Phone app it needs your call history (for Recents and missed calls), contacts (names, photos and favourites) and phone (to make and receive calls). Everything stays on your phone and is never sent anywhere; the app can't even connect to the internet.

Is it really free?

Yes. No ads, no in-app purchases, no subscription.

Which phones does it work on?

Android 12 or newer: Pixel, Samsung, OnePlus, Vivo, Xiaomi and more, including tablets and foldables.

How do I send feedback?

Open Dial Deck › Settings › Help › Send feedback. It opens your email app with the details filled in.
